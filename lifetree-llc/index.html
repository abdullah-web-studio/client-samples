About LifeTree Property Solutions

LifeTree LLC trades as LifeTree Property Solutions — a residential and commercial real estate investing company. For 10+ years the firm has been purchasing houses all over Texas, specialising in distressed single-family houses, including foreclosures and bank REOs.
