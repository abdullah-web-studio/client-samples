Frequently asked questions

Do you cater for children?

With pleasure. Children under 6 stay free in the family room and cottage, and we can add a cot or extra bed on request — mention it in your enquiry.

Is there secure parking for a loaded vehicle?

Yes — off-street parking inside the gate is included, and the property is walled with night lighting. Many guests leave vehicles loaded overnight on the way to Chobe.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes — vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free breakfasts are available. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will be ready for you.

Do you offer laundry and long-stay rates?

Laundry can be arranged during your stay, and reduced rates apply for stays of 5 nights or more — ask us on the message form for a long-stay quote.

How do I change or cancel an enquiry?

Simply reply to your confirmation message or use the form above. Changes are free, and cancellation is free up to 48 hours before check-in.
